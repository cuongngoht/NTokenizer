- (technique) started Assumption Reversal (explainer angles that flip common beliefs)
- (idea by coach) Assumption "prediction means guessing" -> reverse: prediction at low loss is the hardest form of understanding; open the article with a compression-game demo
- (idea by coach) Assumption "the model outputs words" -> reverse: it outputs a full probability distribution; the word is our sampling choice
- (idea by coach) Assumption "randomness makes it unreliable" -> reverse: randomness lives only in sampling; show T=0 determinism
- (idea by coach) Assumption "it thinks one token at a time" -> reverse: internal planning (rhyme example) means it looks ahead
- (idea by coach) Assumption "it retrieves facts like a database" -> reverse: facts are reconstructed from distributed features, which is why rare facts break
- (idea by coach) Assumption "more data = memorizing more" -> reverse: more data forces compression into rules (grokking story)
- (idea by coach) Assumption "the model knows how it reasons" -> reverse: the 36+59 case shows its explanation is a story, not a trace
- (idea by coach) Assumption "chain-of-thought shows its reasoning" -> reverse: CoT is a scratchpad that helps but is only 25-39% faithful
- (idea by coach) Assumption "hallucination is a bug" -> reverse: it is the expected output of a calibrated predictor on singleton facts
- (idea by coach) Assumption "bigger is always better" -> reverse: a 1.3B instruction-tuned model beat 175B GPT-3 for users
- (idea by coach) Assumption "prediction cannot be creative" -> reverse: sampling from a sharp-but-wide distribution is controlled creativity
- (idea by coach) Assumption "tokens are words" -> reverse: show how a Vietnamese sentence splits into BPE pieces and why that matters
- (idea by coach) Assumption "accuracy comes from pretraining" -> reverse: for modern assistants, RL with verifiable rewards does much of the work
- (idea by coach) Assumption "the model sees the whole sentence" -> reverse: causal mask means it never sees the future; show the mask matrix
- (idea by coach) Assumption "emergence is magic" -> reverse: it is often a metric artifact, but sometimes a real loss threshold
- (idea by coach) Assumption "a probe finding a world model proves it exists" -> reverse: Vafa et al. show probes can be fooled
- (idea by coach) Assumption "errors are random" -> reverse: errors track training-data probability (embers of autoregression)
- (idea by coach) Assumption "next-token loss is a weak signal" -> reverse: every token is a supervised example, trillions of labels for free

- (idea by coach) Assumption "English and Vietnamese work the same" -> reverse: tonal syllables and tokenizer splits change difficulty
