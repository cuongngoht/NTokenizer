## Synthesis for the decision (inference, not evidence)
- Since 2026-04-01, Vietnam has a statutory TDM/AI-training limitation (IP Law Art. 7(5) plus Decree 134/2026). As reported, it favours a **non-commercial research/training** use of **lawfully published, publicly accessible** news. Conditions reportedly include lawful acquisition and no unreasonable prejudice to rights holders. A hobby/research LM appears to fall closer to the permitted zone than a commercial one. This is unverified until the decree's full text is read.
- The exception covers *use for training*. Nothing found suggests it covers **redistributing article text** (for example, publishing a scraped corpus). Most full articles remain protected works (claims 1–2). Publishing raw text is the high-risk act. Publishing weights or derived statistics is lower risk, though this too is unverified.
- Whether a robots.txt or ToS prohibition makes acquisition "unlawful" under Decree 134 is **unknown**. Honoring robots.txt and AI-bot disallows is the conservative reading.
- Low-risk path: (a) use existing corpora with stated licences (Wikipedia CC BY-SA; CulturaX/OSCAR/C4-vi derived from Common Crawl, which honors CCBot robots rules); (b) if crawling, keep it non-commercial, rate-limited, robots-compliant, and do not redistribute text (store URLs/hashes, release weights only); (c) prefer sites with explicit open licences, such as some government portals (not verified this run).
