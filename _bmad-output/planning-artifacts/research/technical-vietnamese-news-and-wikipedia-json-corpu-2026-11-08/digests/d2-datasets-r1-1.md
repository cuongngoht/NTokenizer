# D2 — Existing Vietnamese news datasets (round 1, agent 1)

Accessed 2026-11-08. Network note: direct fetches to huggingface.co, data.statmt.org, wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de and aclanthology.org failed (DNS blocked in this session), and GitHub API access to third-party repos was refused. Most evidence below comes from search-engine snippets of primary pages (dataset cards, papers). The snippets were not read in full, so confidence is capped at medium unless stated.

## Claims

1. VietNews (Nguyen et al. 2019) has 150,704 news articles, each a summary/abstract + body pair, from tuoitre.vn, vnexpress.net and nguoiduatin.vn. Questionnaires, analytical comments and weather forecasts were removed. | ViT5 paper, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.06457 (search snippet) | VietAI / arXiv | 2022-05 | accessed 2026-11-08 | medium | size
2. The original VietNews repo is github.com/ThanhChinhBK/vietnews ("dataset for Vietnamese summarization benchmarking"; one `data` folder, 1 commit). The repo page shows no license. | GitHub repo page, https://github.com/ThanhChinhBK/vietnews | GitHub | n/a | accessed 2026-11-08 | medium (fetched; the page has no license section) | license
3. A Hugging Face mirror, harouzie/vietnews, labels itself Apache-2.0 (100K–1M rows). No original-release license was found, so this label is the mirror uploader's claim. | https://huggingface.co/datasets/harouzie/vietnews (search snippet) | Hugging Face | n/a | accessed 2026-11-08 | low (as a statement of the real terms) | license
4. binhvq News Corpus (github.com/binhvq/news-corpus) is described as ~20M articles from diverse sources, last updated May 2021. Mirrors give different row counts: vietgpt/binhvq_news_vi has 19,365,593 examples; hieunguyen1053/binhvq-news-corpus has 13,954,498. BigScience also hosted a ROOTS subset (bigscience-data/roots_vi_binhvq_news_corpus). | BKAINewsCorpus README + vietgpt/hieunguyen1053 cards, https://huggingface.co/datasets/bkai-foundation-models/BKAINewsCorpus ; https://huggingface.co/datasets/vietgpt/binhvq_news_vi (search snippets) | BKAI / Hugging Face | 2023–2024 | accessed 2026-11-08 | medium | size
5. BKAINewsCorpus extends binhvq with ~10M more articles up to Nov 2023, for ~32M articles in total. After fuzzy deduplication it is 53 GB of clean text, intended for LLM continual pretraining. The card asks users to cite arXiv 2403.01616. No license field was seen in the card snippets. | BKAINewsCorpus README, https://huggingface.co/datasets/bkai-foundation-models/BKAINewsCorpus/blob/main/README.md (search snippet) | BKAI (HUST) | 2024 | accessed 2026-11-08 | medium (size) / low (license absent) | size, license
6. VNTC (Hoang et al., RIVF 2007; github.com/duyvuleo/VNTC) is a Vietnamese news text-classification corpus with 10 topics (chinh_tri_xa_hoi, doi_song, khoa_hoc, kinh_doanh, phap_luat, suc_khoe, the_gioi, the_thao, van_hoa, vi_tinh). A derivative model card reports 84,132 articles. The data is old (circa 2007) and the license was not found. It can be downloaded through `underthesea download-data VNTC`. | https://github.com/congphuc/VNTC ; https://huggingface.co/BaoNhan/mbert-VNTC-10 (search snippets) | GitHub / Hugging Face | 2007 (paper) | accessed 2026-11-08 | medium (topics) / low (size) | size, practice
7. VLSP 2020 MT shared task released 2M Vietnamese sentences crawled from news sites as monolingual data. | VLSP 2020 paper, https://preview.aclanthology.org/fix_video/2020.vlsp-1.18.pdf (search snippet) | VLSP / ACL Anthology | 2020-12 | accessed 2026-11-08 | low-medium | size
8. PhoBERT-style pretraining used ~19 GB of filtered text taken from a ~40 GB Vietnamese news corpus, plus Wikipedia. This is very likely the binhvq corpus (the corpus was not named in the snippet). | search snippet citing arXiv 2003.00744 (PhoBERT), https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.00744v1.pdf | VinAI / arXiv | 2020-03 | accessed 2026-11-08 | medium | size, practice
9. Leipzig Corpora Collection: Le & Quasthoff (LREC 2016) describe a Vietnamese corpus of ~4.05 billion words built from Wikipedia, newspaper articles and random web text. Their paper says the data is available for online search and for download. | "Construction and Analysis of a Large Vietnamese Text Corpus", https://aclanthology.org/L16-1065/ (search snippet) | ELRA / ACL Anthology | 2016-05 | accessed 2026-11-08 | medium | size

## Leads

- Read the BKAINewsCorpus YAML header for its license field, and arXiv 2403.01616 for source sites and years. This needs huggingface.co / arxiv.org to be reachable.
- binhvq/news-corpus README: original license/terms, the list of source sites, and its withdrawal status. This needs GitHub access through add_repo or raw.githubusercontent.com; the README path guessed for vietnews returned 404.
- statmt.org News Crawl `vi` per-year files and the WMT terms ("research purposes only"; this is an unverified belief). Needs data.statmt.org to be allowed.
- Leipzig download page for Vietnamese: corpus names (e.g. `vie_news_*`, `vie_newscrawl_*`), years and sentence counts, and the actual terms. Needs wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de to be allowed.
- FineWeb-2 / CulturaX dataset cards for any per-domain statistics, or sample the `url` field directly to estimate the share of vnexpress.net and dantri.com.vn.
- Search on Hugging Face for "vietnamese news" sets from vietgpt and undertheseanlp, and check their licenses.
