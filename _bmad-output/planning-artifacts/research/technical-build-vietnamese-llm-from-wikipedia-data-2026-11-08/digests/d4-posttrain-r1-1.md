### Q2 — SFT/DPO on tiny models
9. SmolLM2-135M-Instruct was built with SFT on public and curated data (SmolTalk; `smol-smoltalk` subset linked for small models), then DPO on UltraFeedback. The card reports IFEval 29.9 / MT-Bench 1.98, against 17.2 / 1.68 for SmolLM-135M-Instruct. Instruction tuning measurably helps at 135M, from a low base. | SmolLM2-135M-Instruct model card (https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M-Instruct) | Hugging Face | 2024-11 | 2026-11-08 | medium (via snippet) | performance, practice
10. SmolLM2 paper recipe (reported for the main run, not confirmed for 135M): SFT 2 epochs, LR 3e-4, seq 8192. DPO LR 1e-6, beta 0.5, seq 1024. UltraFeedback was the most consistently effective preference set. | "SmolLM2: When Smol Goes Big" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02737) | Hugging Face (arXiv) | 2025-02 | 2026-11-08 | medium | practice
11. TinyStories: models under 10M params trained on synthetic simple stories produce fluent text. An instruction-following variant was evaluated with GPT-4 as grader (grammar, creativity, consistency, instruction-following). A secondary summary says instruction-following emerges with about 2+ layers and that 1-layer models struggle (not verified against the PDF). | TinyStories (https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07759) | Microsoft Research | 2023-05 | 2026-11-08 | high (core), low (layer finding) | performance, practice
12. Loss masking: masking prompt tokens is the default in common SFT libraries. Shi et al. (NeurIPS 2024) find that also computing loss on instruction tokens ("Instruction Modelling") improves results, most when outputs are short relative to instructions and in low-data SFT (relevant to small Vietnamese SFT sets). Chat special tokens (`<|user|>` etc.) stay masked in their setup. | "Instruction Tuning With Loss Over Instructions" (https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/7ffb43adf37b3eeaba559098bc084cc6-Abstract.html); Raschka commentary (https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/llm-research-insights-instruction) | NeurIPS / Ahead of AI | 2024-05 | 2026-11-08 | high | practice
13. DPO at small scale is feasible in practice: SmolLM2-135M-Instruct shipped with a DPO stage (claim 9). Vietnamese preference pairs exist only as MT (5CD-AI collection, claim 5). | as 9, 5 | — | — | 2026-11-08 | medium | practice

### Q4 — Sampling-time quick wins
18. Min-p sampling scales the truncation threshold by the top token's probability, with claimed better coherence at high temperature. ICLR 2025 oral. Adopted in HF transformers and vLLM. | "Turning Up the Heat: Min-p Sampling…" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01082 ; https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/oral/31888) | ICLR | 2024-07 | 2026-11-08 | high (existence/adoption) | practice
19. A critique ("Min-p, Max Exaggeration", NeurIPS 2026 listing) argues min-p is largely indistinguishable from other samplers within 95% CIs and that the original human evaluation had statistical and reporting flaws. Treat min-p's gains as contested. | arXiv 2506.13681 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13681); https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/166291 | Schaeffer et al. | 2025-06 | 2026-11-08 | high | performance
20. Contrastive decoding (expert minus amateur log-probs; OPT-13B vs OPT-125M) beats nucleus and top-k on automatic and human evaluation, because small LMs exhibit repetition and incoherence more. It needs a larger expert, so it is of limited use when your model IS the small one. A follow-up reports smaller amateurs help. | "Contrastive Decoding: Open-ended Text Generation as Optimization" ACL 2023 (https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.687) ; arXiv 2309.09117 | ACL | 2023-07 | 2026-11-08 | high | performance
